SOAD-N130 Digital Imagery · Apr – May 2020

A house as a Swiss Army knife.

A depiction of what home felt like during COVID: a pixel-art GIF in which a plant pot keeps growing and a Swiss Army knife keeps unfolding new tools.

Role
Creator · Designer
Type
Individual · Academic
Duration
4 weeks
Toolkit
Photoshop · Illustrator · Pixel Art
The idea Craft

The idea

The plant pot is a metaphor for the house as a perpetually growing entity. The Swiss Army knife is the foundation — it embodies all the different tools and appliances inside the house that suddenly had to do everything during lockdown.

Since the knife is the foundation for all the movement in the GIF, it sits dead-center so the eye stays on what's unfolding above it.

Craft

Drawn as pixel art in Photoshop and Illustrator and assembled frame-by-frame into a looping GIF. The constraint of a tiny canvas forced every element to earn its pixels.
