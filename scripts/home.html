How this page is put together

Quiet, Poppins for words, real work as the picture.

Color

Color follows the content, so the page stays neutral

Read more

Light, then dark. Tokens from v-stamp.css.

  • ink text#111110 / #f4efe4
  • ink-2 secondary text#5f5b53 / #a8a296
  • paper the page#fbf8f1 / #121211
  • line hairlinesrgba(17,17,16,.12) / rgba(244,239,228,.10)
  • accent links, active tab, focus ring#4a3fd1 / #9b86ff
  • seal the endorsement square#c63d2f / #e0533f
  • live status dot, nothing else#30d158 / #30d158
Stamp Ink, light: #f3ecdd to #f7f2e7 to #fbf8f1, a faint warm wash settling into paper
Night Press, dark: #1d1b18 to #121211, warm near-black

Each project sits on its own soft gradient stage, tinted from its build mark, so the work supplies the color. Everything else stays neutral: warm paper, ink, and the same seal red as my name stamp, so the page and the stamp read as one hand.

One accent does the rest of the work. A deep violet marks links, back arrows, the active tab and focus rings. Green appears in one place only: the dot that means something is live. Text and links clear 4.5:1 on paper and on every card tint.

Type

Poppins for words, a plain monospace for facts

Read more

400, 500 and 600 are loaded. I kept Poppins instead of switching to the SF system font. Its round, geometric letters feel friendly, not corporate. One family for every word keeps the focus on the work, and it has carried every version of this site.

Structure

Pages you explore, with the work as the picture

Read more
  • Pill tabs for Builds, Work, Writing and About, routed by the URL hash. Builds opens first.
  • Builds shows three projects to try, then five folders. Each opens in a tray, so the page stays short.
  • Every project is a framed artifact: a real screenshot, a card, a gauge.
  • A live Berkeley clock and a day or night icon in the footer.
  • Light and dark themes, saved in the browser.
  • Press ⌘K (Ctrl K on Windows) to jump to any build, tab or page.
  • Every build page has a row of build marks to hop between builds, and a contents list that follows your scroll.

Shapes and motion

  • Cards 24px radius. Content width 1200px.
  • Hover easing cubic-bezier(.32, .72, 0, 1).
  • Frosted blur on small tiles only. Everything else is a plain surface with a hairline.
  • Motion is slow and soft, plays once, and switches off when your device asks for less motion.
