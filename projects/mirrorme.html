An icebreaker card game for people who host. 150+ pre-launch purchase commitments at a $30 to 40 price point, with zero paid budget.

The problem

Hosts want their guests to actually talk. Most icebreaker games are either too shallow to matter or too intense for a room of near-strangers.

What I did

  • 100+ user surveys and a 10-product competitive scan identified two high-intent segments: regular hosts, and the venues that host them.
  • Designed the deck around the OCEAN personality model: category cards set a trait, question cards ask about someone at the table, and players rate 1 to 5.
  • Skipped paid channels. Sold in person at card game cafes and social events in New York.
  • Created and sold 50+ decks of the original game.

Results

150+pre-launch purchase commitments
$30 to 40price point
$0paid budget
50+decks sold

Insight: it sold itself in rooms where people already wanted to connect, so venue partnerships became the core channel.
