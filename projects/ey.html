The problem

A Fortune 500 life sciences client was migrating its application portfolio to the cloud across 25+ teams. Risks surfaced late, executives got escalations instead of decisions, and there was no shared view of what was on track.

What I did

  • Established a program-wide risk and OKR system by implementing blocker and risk triage across 25+ teams, which enabled earlier resource reallocation and reduced executive escalations by 30%.
  • Designed and launched the client's first QBR by synthesizing 50+ executive interviews into an operational roadmap, then standardized QBR governance across 5+ transformation programs.
  • Orchestrated enterprise-wide adoption of a custom program management methodology by managing a 15-person team and designing and running tool training. 90% adoption among 4,000 target users.
  • Prioritized applications for migration, modernization, or retirement across the client's IT portfolio with a value-capture framework. Secured SVP approval and identified $10M+ in savings over 3+ years.
